Make OFFMODE
your own.

A practical setup and customization guide for OFFMODE, a Shopify clothing theme built around campaign storytelling, product discovery, and shopping.

Get started

  1. In Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes. Find OFFMODE and select Customize.
  2. Under Products, add your products, images, prices, inventory, and size or color variants. Create collections to organize them.
  3. Configure your store name, policies, payments, shipping, taxes, and markets in Shopify admin. These are store settings, not theme settings.
  4. Preview your store on desktop and mobile. Check the complete shopping journey before publishing.
Tip: Duplicate your theme before making extensive changes to preserve a backup.

Logo, colors, fonts & social links

In the theme editor, open Theme settings. Use Logo and favicon for your brand graphics, Typography for fonts, and Colors to adjust background, foreground, accent, and highlight colors.

In Social media, add links to your Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook profiles, then select Save.

Check text and button contrast after changing colors. Add meaningful alternative text to product and campaign images in Shopify.

Customize the home page

Select Home page in the theme editor. Add, hide, remove, or reorder the available sections.

  • Campaign hero: Edit eyebrow, heading, description, campaign image, button label, and button link. Without a selected link, the button leads to the full product catalog.
  • Featured products: Choose a collection, heading, and product count. Without a selected collection, the section uses the all-products collection.
  • Editorial campaign: Add story-driven text, imagery, and a linked call to action.
  • Custom Liquid: Add Liquid in available supported insertion points. Preview carefully before publishing.

Click Save to keep your changes.

Header & footer

Open Header in the theme editor to select a main menu and customer-account menu. Create or edit navigation menus in Shopify admin first.

The footer includes a newsletter signup, optional menu, payment icons, social links, and country or language selectors when multiple choices are available in your Shopify store configuration.

Products & collections

Create products and variants in Shopify admin. Product pages present media, descriptions, variants, prices, and purchasing controls. Depending on configured product data, they may include color swatches, selling plans, gift-card recipient fields, pickup availability, and complementary recommendations.

In the theme editor, open a product page and expand Main product to manage blocks such as title, price, description, buy buttons, pickup availability, complementary products, app blocks, and Custom Liquid.

Optional features: Pickup information requires eligible locations and pickup settings. Complementary products require setup in Shopify Search & Discovery. Blocks without applicable data may not appear.

Collection pages support available sorting and filters. Configure filters using Shopify Search & Discovery, and add collection images and descriptions through Shopify admin.

Pages, contact & FAQ templates

In Shopify admin, create the content pages you need and select their available theme templates. OFFMODE includes default, About, Contact, and FAQ page templates.

The Contact template contains a merchant storefront contact form. To edit the FAQ page, open its FAQ section in the theme editor and update questions and answers. Review all sample content and store policies before launch.

Important: Your store's Contact page is for store customers. Arky Labs must also provide a separate public support form for OFFMODE theme merchants.

Search, cart & checkout

The search page supports predictive suggestions and available Shopify filters. Shoppers can update cart quantities, remove items, add an order note, and continue to checkout.

Checkout and payment methods are controlled by Shopify store settings, not the theme editor.

Troubleshooting

  • Product not visible: Ensure it is active, available to the Online Store channel, and in the selected collection.
  • Product sold out: Check variant inventory, channel availability, and inventory policy.
  • Missing menu or image: Select the correct resource in the relevant theme settings, and save.
  • Pickup or complementary items missing: Check the associated Shopify configurations and product eligibility.
  • Changes not visible: Save settings and confirm you are previewing the intended theme and template.

Frequently asked questions

Can I customize OFFMODE without writing code?

Yes. The theme editor provides controls for supported sections, blocks, theme settings, and menus. Advanced changes may require Liquid, CSS, or development assistance.

Why are complementary products not appearing?

Set up complementary recommendations through Shopify Search & Discovery and verify that the relevant product has recommendations assigned.

Why are pickup options not visible?

Confirm that pickup is enabled for an eligible location and that the selected variant has applicable pickup data.

How do I change the products on my home page?

Open the Featured products section in the theme editor, select your collection, and choose the number of products to display.

Does OFFMODE control my store's checkout?

No. Checkout and payment setup are managed through Shopify.
